<p><strong>Welcome to the LangChain Forum</strong>  –  a space for developers to ask questions and connect with others building with LangChain products.</p>
<p>Looking for broader conversation, events, or want to showcase your agents? Join our <strong><a href="https://www.langchain.com/join-community">LangChain Slack Community</a></strong>.</p>
<p>Before you dive in, here’s what to know:</p>
<ul>
<li>Keep discussions <strong>focused on technical topics related to LangChain</strong>. Promotional content or solicitation of any kind will result in an immediate ban.</li>
<li>Before starting a new topic, please <strong>search</strong> to see if the topic has already been discussed.</li>
<li>If you are helping someone, <strong>do</strong> <strong>not</strong> answer by linking to an external link (unless it is the LangChain Docs), as external articles may be incorrect and are impossible for us to update.</li>
</ul>
<p>By participating, you agree to follow our Community Code of Conduct. Read the full guidelines <strong><a href="https://www.langchain.com/community-code">here</a></strong>.</p>
