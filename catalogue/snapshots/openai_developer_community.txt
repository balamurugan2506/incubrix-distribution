    <h1><a name="p-6-guidelines-1" class="anchor" href="#p-6-guidelines-1" aria-label="Heading link"></a>Guidelines</h1>
<p>Thank you for using and building with OpenAI products and developer tools. This forum is a space for developers, users, and community members to meet, learn, share, and help one another. ChatGPT questions, bug reports, feedback, feature requests, use cases, and troubleshooting are welcome in the appropriate Community Forum category.</p>
<p>This is a public, community-run forum. Posting here does not create a private support case, and a response or response time from OpenAI staff is not guaranteed.</p>
<p>We cannot support individual billing issues or account suspension, deactivation, ban, or appeal requests on the public forum. These cases require personal, account-specific, or payment details that cannot be safely collected or reviewed here. For billing issues, visit <a href="https://help.openai.com">help.openai.com</a> and open the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner to contact Support. For an account suspension, deactivation, ban, or appeal, use the appeal link in your notification email. If you cannot access it, submit the <a href="https://openai.com/form/appeal/">appeal form</a>. You can also contact Support at <a href="https://help.openai.com">help.openai.com</a> by opening the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner.</p>
<h2><a name="p-6-openai-developer-community-2" class="anchor" href="#p-6-openai-developer-community-2" aria-label="Heading link"></a>OpenAI Developer Community</h2>
<p><a href="https://community.openai.com/">The OpenAI Developer Community</a> is a space where developers, users, and community members can learn, share, collaborate, and get community support while using and building with OpenAI products and developer tools. It is a great way to see what others are working on, trade tips, hear about the latest product updates, and share feedback.</p>
<h2><a name="p-6-please-3" class="anchor" href="#p-6-please-3" aria-label="Heading link"></a>Please:</h2>
<p>Choose the most relevant <a href="https://community.openai.com/categories">category</a> before posting. Questions, technical discussions, bug reports, feedback, feature requests, and use cases are welcome when they fit the category’s purpose.</p>
<p>Use a clear, descriptive title and helpful tags. Keep one issue per topic.</p>
<p>For bugs or troubleshooting questions, include reproduction steps, expected vs actual behavior, product/model/version, platform or environment, timestamp and timezone, request ID if relevant, and sanitized logs.</p>
<p>Be friendly, welcoming, respectful, and constructive in your communication with fellow community members.</p>
<p>Be collaborative. Share knowledge, prompt tips/tricks, patterns, solutions, and helpful context with fellow community members.</p>
<p>Search our <a href="https://help.openai.com/en/">Help Center</a>, product documentation, <a href="https://status.openai.com/">status page</a>, and the <a href="https://community.openai.com/search?expanded=true">Community Forum</a> before sharing a new question related to ChatGPT, the API, or developer tools.</p>
<p>Keep it clean. No profanity, obscenity, or insulting comments. If you need to share a prompt or output that is otherwise permitted but may be sensitive or offensive, include a clear trigger warning and redact unnecessary details.</p>
<p>Share the cool things you have built, but avoid repetitive or overly promotional posts. Contributions should be relevant to OpenAI products, developer work, or the category’s purpose.</p>
<h2><a name="p-6-please-dont-4" class="anchor" href="#p-6-please-dont-4" aria-label="Heading link"></a>Please don’t:</h2>
<p>Post individual billing issues or account suspension, deactivation, ban, or appeal requests. These issues require private account or payment details and cannot be handled on the public forum. For billing issues, visit <a href="https://help.openai.com">help.openai.com</a> and open the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner to contact Support. For an account suspension, deactivation, ban, or appeal, use the appeal link in your notification email. If you cannot access it, submit the <a href="https://openai.com/form/appeal/">appeal form</a>. You can also contact Support at <a href="https://help.openai.com">help.openai.com</a> by opening the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner.</p>
<p>Share sensitive or personal information, including API keys, passwords, access tokens, session cookies, payment details, account credentials, personal or customer data, or unredacted screenshots, logs, or code.</p>
<p>Impersonate OpenAI, OpenAI staff, moderators, or other community members. Do not falsely claim to represent OpenAI, solicit credentials, or ask members to send sensitive account information by direct message.</p>
<p>Insult or put down other community members. Harassment and sexist, racist, or exclusionary jokes will not be tolerated. Sexual language and imagery are not tolerated.</p>
<p>Cross-post the same issue in multiple topics or categories.</p>
<p>Promote or advertise services unrelated to OpenAI products, developer work, or the category’s purpose. Spamming other members with pitches or other forms of self-promotion will not be tolerated.</p>
<p>Divert a forum topic by changing it midstream. Instead, start a new topic or use the “Reply as a Linked Topic” feature.</p>
<p>If you have any questions or suggestions for improving the community spaces, open a new topic in the <a href="https://community.openai.com/c/forum-feedback/22">Forum feedback category</a> and let’s discuss!</p>
<h2><a name="p-6-termination-and-cancellation-5" class="anchor" href="#p-6-termination-and-cancellation-5" aria-label="Heading link"></a>Termination and Cancellation</h2>
<p>The above guidelines apply to all OpenAI online spaces and channels. OpenAI reserves the right to review and remove community content and applications at will and without notice. Anyone who violates the above guidelines may be expelled from the community or have their access terminated.</p>
